## Gate stopped being just "gate io" a while ago

The platform launched in 2013 as Bter, renamed itself Gate.io in late 2017, and did a full brand refresh in 2025 under the domain Gate.com with a Chinese name, 大门. Founder and CEO Dr. Han still runs it. If you're comparing it to other venues, the relevant scale numbers from its own listings and CoinMarketCap's exchange page are: more than 60 million users, over 5,200 digital assets, and 12,800+ stock and ETF products on the equity side. CoinMarketCap currently ranks its 24-hour spot volume second globally.

Check your own jurisdiction before doing anything else — a bonus you can't withdraw isn't a bonus.

Guidance: trust the fee page, and verify the number on the day you trade, since Gate restructured spot and futures fees on 9 April 2026.

Three details matter more than the headline rate:

- **Makers get nothing extra for four tiers.** From VIP 0 to VIP 3, maker and taker cost exactly the same. If your style is resting limit orders, Gate charges you for providing liquidity until VIP 4.

- **USDC/USDT costs 0% on both sides.** As a stablecoin swap it's about as cheap as it gets — but that pair's volume doesn't count toward your VIP tier.

### Where the tier system quietly bites

Four things are easy to miss until they cost you money.

First, volume isn't counted equally. Spot and stock trading count 100%, USDT and BTC perpetuals count 40%, options and USD1 contracts count 20%, and CFD contracts count 10%. A futures desk needs 2.5× the notional turnover of a spot trader to land on the same tier, and a CFD trader ten times.

Second, the GT discount stops helping exactly where fees get interesting. At VIP 0 it saves you 0.01 percentage points; from VIP 10 upward the GT rate and the standard rate are the same number, so holding the token buys you nothing at the tiers where the volume is.

Third, GT deduction spends your GT first and then falls back to the standard rate the moment the balance runs dry — silently. Budget a session at 0.09% and empty your GT halfway through, and the rest bills at 0.10%.

Fourth, withdrawal limits are tier-bound. A new VIP 0 account gets a $3M rolling 24-hour limit, which is plenty for most people and a hard wall for a few.

## The sign-up bonus: what you actually have to do

This is the section most "gate io bonus" pages get wrong, either by inflating it or by describing expired campaigns. Here's how the Rewards Hub is structured right now.

The newcomer package is a list of one-time tasks. You register, verify identity including a face check, make a first deposit, make a first spot or futures trade, and download the app. Completing all of them pays out a package that regional versions of the page value somewhere above 100 USDT — the exact figure differs by country (variants I found quoted 118, 135, 160 and 165 USDT), which tells you it's assembled on the local offers rather than being one global number. A chunk of it comes as vouchers and position-experience coupons rather than withdrawable cash. Some tasks pay 15 USDT, others a coupon.

Conditions that decide whether you get paid:

- New-user tasks must be completed within 15 days of registering
- Newcomer rewards are claimed manually; the larger challenge rewards are distributed automatically, typically within 48 hours after a 3-day review period
- Sub-accounts don't qualify, and volume traded on them doesn't count toward the main account
- Institutional, API and affiliate accounts are excluded — retail only
- Stablecoin trading volume doesn't count toward Rewards Hub volume
- Users who deactivated an account and re-registered under the same identity don't get newcomer status again
- Fraud, wash trading and matched orders result in disqualification

The headline number on the hub is a challenge framework that unlocks in stages as you hit net-deposit and cumulative-volume thresholds, up to 10,000 USDT (a couple of regional versions show 15,000). It's a ladder, not a lump sum. You unlock 10 USDT, then more as you clear each stage.

## The GT token, in one paragraph

GT is the exchange token, and its role in your fee bill is narrower than the marketing implies. It gives you a 0.01-point discount at the base tier, counts toward the VIP track via your 14-day average holding, and has been deflationary since GateChain launched in 2019 — over 189 million GT burned, roughly 63% of the original supply, including 2.57 million GT (about $17.75M) in the Q2 2026 burn. Useful if you trade enough for a tenth of a basis point to matter. Irrelevant if you don't.

## Who Gate suits, and who should look elsewhere

It's a strong fit if your problem is **coverage**. 5,200+ crypto assets means new listings show up there earlier than on venues with stricter listing committees, and the tokenised-stock side — 10,000+ US equities and ETFs, 1,500+ Hong Kong names, 1,000+ Korean names, tradable around the clock with fractional sizing from 0.01 of a share — is a genuinely unusual offering for a crypto exchange. Add copy trading, trading bots, Alpha, Launchpool, Gate Pay and a Web3 wallet, and it covers a lot of surface.

It's a weaker fit in a few specific cases:

- **You work limit orders and nothing else.** At VIP 0 to VIP 3 Gate charges makers the same as takers. Exchanges with a standing 0% maker rate will bill you less for identical behaviour.
- **You only trade a handful of majors and care about depth.** On obscure pairs, spreads and slippage are wider than on the largest venues — that's the trade-off for a longer listing list.
- **You're in a restricted region.** No amount of bonus changes that.
- **You want a flawless mobile app.** Google Play reviews of the Gate app repeatedly complain about forced update prompts and periodic freezes, with support replies confirming the feedback was logged. Not fatal, but worth knowing before you plan to manage positions from a phone.

One practical note for anyone deciding: these are leveraged products, and the BENUSDT episode showed how quickly a contract-level error can hit accounts that had nothing to do with the trade. Position sizing matters more than fee tiers.
